## 2. Naya: Classes tab

Dashboard → **Classes**. Yeh poori app ka *source of truth* hai.

Har class ke liye:
- **Monthly fee** (Rs) — student add karte waqt fee isi se tay hoti hai
- **Subjects** — us class ke kon kon se subjects hain
- Har subject ke **marks totals**: default total + Mid / Final / Weekly / Monthly

12 classes pehle se bani hain (Nursery, KG, 1st – 10th) — **fee aur subjects
jaan boojh kar khaali chhore gaye hain**, aap bharein ge.

Subjects ke bina exam nahi ban sakta, is liye pehle Classes mein subjects add
karein, phir Exams.

## 5. Fee tab

- **Search**: student ka naam ya roll no likhein
- Har student ke saamne **kitna baqi** hai (ya "Clear")
- Detail screen pe:
  - Monthly fee, **Payable / Paid / Balance**
  - **Kitne mahinon ki fee baqi** hai aur kon kon se
  - Month-by-month list: Paid / Partial / Pending
  - Payments ki receipts (delete bhi ho sakti hain)
- **Collect Fee**: mahina chunein, amount likhein — **installment** ke liye kam
  amount bhi chalega, baqi usi mahine ke against baqi rahega
  (quick buttons: Poora mahina / Baqi / Aadha)

### Fee ka model
Class ki fee **monthly** hai. Har mahine ka hisaab apne aap banta hai:

```
payable  = monthly_fee × (mahinon ki tadaad)
paid     = us student ki tamam payments ka jama
balance  = payable − paid
```

- Purane students ka hisaab **is mahine se** shuru hota hai (admission date se
  backfill nahi) — aap ki pasand ke mutabiq.
- Naye student ka hisaab us ke **admission month** se shuru hota hai.
- Purani `fees` table ki jama raqam migration mein `fee_payments` mein chali
  gayi hai (note ke saath: "Purane record se migrate hua").
